# SillyTavern on Nimi

Create character conversations using character cards, your Persona and world books. Keep and edit chats, explore alternative replies and use configured media capabilities alongside conversation.

## Start a conversation

1. Open SillyTavern from Nimi Home and keep Nimi running.
2. Choose the Nimi AI resources needed for the conversation.
3. Create or import a character card, select your Persona and start chatting. Add world-book context when the story needs it.
4. Use the available chat controls to edit, continue, stop or explore another reply. Export important characters and conversations through the App's normal controls.

Image generation, speech and retrieval need their corresponding configured capabilities. Model output can contradict a character or world description; review it before treating it as established story content.

## Data and audience

Characters, conversations and attachments use SillyTavern's local App data. Requests sent through a Cloud configuration include the relevant conversation and supplied context. Tools that search or fetch websites also access those external services.

This App is intended for adults. Content depends on the cards, world books and models you choose. Keep private information out of shared character cards and exported conversations.

For help, contact the App provider with the affected feature and error message, excluding personal chats and credentials.
